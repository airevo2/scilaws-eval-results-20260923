    ln(alpha) = b0 + b1 * x + b2 * x^2

    both younger-adult and older ages.

    b0 = -1.20
    b1 = 0.0585
    b2 = -0.000489

    return b0 + b1 * x + b2 * x**2

    t = X[:, 1].astype(float)

    xc = x - 60.0
    tc = t - 1950.0

    b0 =  0.55      # peak level of log sex ratio near age 60
    b1 = -0.004     # near-flat slope at the peak
    b2 = -0.00035   # downward curvature (convergence at old ages)
    b3 =  0.0015    # slight secular increase over calendar time

    return b0 + b1 * xc + b2 * xc**2 + b3 * tc
