import numpy as np

def predict(X):
    """
    Predict the ratio G_E / G_D for the proton electric Sachs form factor.
    By the standard dipole approximation, the proton electric form factor
    is equal to the dipole form factor, so the ratio is unity.
    """
    Q2 = X[:, 0]
    return np.ones_like(Q2)

    In the standard dipole model, the proton electric Sachs form factor
    G_E is identical to the dipole form factor G_D:
        G_D(Q^2) = (1 + Q^2 / 0.71 GeV^2)^{-2}
    Therefore the ratio G_E / G_D = 1 for all Q^2.
    """

    return np.ones_like(Q2)
