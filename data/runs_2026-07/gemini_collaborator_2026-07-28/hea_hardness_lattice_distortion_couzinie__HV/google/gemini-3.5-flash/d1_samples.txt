    atomic-size mismatch, valence electron concentration, and mixing enthalpy.

    Inputs:

        - X[:, 1] = VEC (valence electron concentration)
        - X[:, 2] = dHmix (ΔH_mix) [kJ/mol] (Miedema enthalpy of mixing)

import numpy as np


def predict(X):
    """
    Predicts the room-temperature Vickers hardness (HV) [kgf/mm²] of a refractory
    or complex-concentrated high-entropy alloy using a semi-empirical relationship
    based on lattice distortion (delta), valence electron concentration (VEC),
    and the enthalpy of mixing (dHmix).

    Inputs:
    - X: 2-D numpy array of shape (N, 3)
         X[:, 0] = delta (atomic-size mismatch) [%]
         X[:, 1] = VEC (valence electron concentration)
         X[:, 2] = dHmix (enthalpy of mixing) [kJ/mol]

    Returns:
    - 1-D numpy array of predicted HV values.
    """
    delta = X[:, 0]
    VEC = X[:, 1]
    dHmix = X[:, 2]

    # Semi-empirical model capturing:
    # 1. Solid-solution strengthening scaling with lattice distortion (delta)
    # 2. Peierls barrier/crystal structure effect via VEC (BCC at low VEC is harder than FCC at high VEC)
    # 3. Short-range ordering/bond-strength enhancement from negative enthalpy of mixing (dHmix)
    HV = 22.0 * delta + 28.0 * (VEC - 8.0) ** 2 - 8.0 * dHmix + 110.0

    return HV
